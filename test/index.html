    Recipe and bookshelf files

    Each backup is one file, a copy of recipes.json or cookbooks.json. To restore, put it back in the app's Dropbox folder under that name. Photos and covers stay in Dropbox, which keeps its own version history of them.

    Exports for meal planning

    Export for meal planning shares both files at once, ready to save to Files or Dropbox or attach to your AI project. The recipes file is your full recipes.json; the Bookshelf CSV has one row per recipe in your cookbooks. Exporting never changes anything in Dropbox.

    Photos on this device

    Photos you've viewed are kept on this device so they open quickly. Clearing them is safe; they download again from Dropbox.

    Unused photos in Dropbox

    Finds photo files no recipe or book uses any more, for example after deleting while offline, and removes them.

    Add contents

    1. Copy the prompt and paste it into your AI with photos of the book's recipe list: the contents page if it lists recipes, otherwise the index at the back. If the contents page only lists chapters, add a photo of it too. A long index can go in batches.

    Each paste adds to the book's contents. Recipes already listed (same title and page) are skipped.
